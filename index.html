The Villa

A neoclassical residence,
reimagined for the Cretan coast

Costa Greca takes its name from the Greek coastline it overlooks. Its architecture draws on the symmetry and grace of neoclassical design: columns, cornices, tall shuttered windows. Inside, the rooms are calm, contemporary and full of light.

This is one of the largest private villas on the north coast of Crete. It is made for families and friends who travel together: generous living rooms, a pergola-shaded dining terrace, landscaped gardens, a private basketball court and a pool at its heart. Up to fourteen guests can share it in comfort and complete privacy.

Spaces

Room to gather, room to retreat

I

The Residence

A limestone facade crowned by a classical pediment, with wrought-iron balconies on every level. The pergola terrace holds a long dining table for evenings outdoors with Cretan wine, local cheeses and fresh seafood from the nearby harbour.

II

Bedrooms & Suites

Serene bedrooms dressed in fine linen sleep up to fourteen guests. Warm wood, marble floors and sculptural lighting create a private retreat, and many rooms look out over the olive trees towards the sea.

III

Pool, Gardens & Court

The walled grounds hold a private pool with sun loungers, a shaded pergola, manicured gardens lined with cypress trees, and a full basketball court. There is room for every age to play, swim and unwind in complete privacy.

Location

Karteros, on the north coast of Crete

Costa Greca is near Karteros Beach, a long stretch of golden sand east of Heraklion. Here you are close to everything: the island's capital, its finest archaeological sites and its tavernas. Yet the villa stays a world apart.

Experiences

The island at your doorstep

Golden beaches

Start the morning on the soft sand of Karteros, then explore the coves and clear water along the north coast.

Minoan heritage

Walk through Knossos, the legendary palace of King Minos. Then see its treasures at the Heraklion Archaeological Museum.

The Cretan table

Taste wine at the vineyards of the Heraklion countryside, then enjoy fresh seafood in a harbourside taverna.
