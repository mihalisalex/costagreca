# Villa photos

Web-ready copies of the villa photos (JPG, about 80% quality). The full-size originals are in `photos-originals/`, which is not uploaded to GitHub.

To add or swap a photo:
1. Export it as a JPG, 1600–2400px wide and under about 800 KB.
2. Save it here with a descriptive name, e.g. `living-room.jpg`.
3. Reference it in `index.html` as `images/living-room.jpg`.

The three photos in the "Experiences" section are still stock images of Greek beaches and food, not the villa.
